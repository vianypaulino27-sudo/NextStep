FEDERAL AID

FAFSA Tutorial

What is the FAFSA? The Free Application for Federal Student Aid is the federal form for Pell Grants, work-study and federal student loans. Many colleges and New York State also use it. It's free, and you file it at StudentAid.gov.

NEW YORK STATE

New York TAP Tutorial

What is TAP? New York's Tuition Assistance Program is a state grant, run by HESC, for eligible New York residents attending approved New York colleges. You start it from the FAFSA.

FAFSA vs. TAP: what's the difference?

  • FAFSA is the federal financial aid application. Almost every student heading to college should file it.
  • TAP is a New York State grant for eligible NY residents at approved NY colleges. You reach the TAP application after you submit the FAFSA (HESC also has a separate "Apply for TAP" option).
  • Verified on HESC's TAP page: awards are $1,000 to $5,665 a year, the income limit for dependent students is $125,000 net taxable income, and you must attend full time. HESC lists June 30, 2028 as the TAP deadline for the 2027-28 school year.
  • FAFSA deadlines differ by state and college, and many come early. See the official FAFSA deadlines page (opens in a new tab).

Keep your accounts safe

Never enter your StudentAid.gov (FSA ID) or HESC password, your Social Security number, or bank details on NextStep. Use them only on the official .gov sites. The FAFSA is always free.
